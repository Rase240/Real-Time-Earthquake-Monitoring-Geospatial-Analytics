Limitations:

* Counts aggregate a whole 10-degree cell; the forecast says nothing about where in the cell, when in the week, or how large any individual earthquake will be.
* Most of the forecast skill comes from persistence of ongoing sequences (recent counts); the model cannot anticipate a sequence that has not started.
* Intervals assume a negative-binomial distribution with one dispersion for all regions; coverage is checked on the test period and varies by region.
* Catalogue completeness at M4.5 varies by region and over time; counts are compared only within each cell, never across cells.
* Live features use the USGS feed at the forecast origin; events posted late lower the most recent count.
